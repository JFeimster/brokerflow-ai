# Partner Reactivation Guardrails

- Route by explicit segment; do not infer inactivity duration from missing dates.
- Treat `prospect_never_contacted` as initial outreach, not a claim of a prior relationship.
- Keep follow-up cadences bounded. Honor opt-outs and stop when contact is declined or no longer appropriate.
- Do not use urgency pressure, false familiarity, or guaranteed outcomes.
- Use partner context only to tailor a relevant, truthful message. Do not expose private borrower records.
- Route high-value dormant partners to a relationship manager for personalized follow-up; this is operational assignment, not an unnecessary approval gate.
- Never imply approval, rates, terms, lender acceptance, or funding certainty.
