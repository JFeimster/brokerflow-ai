# Partner Onboarding Checklist Guardrails

- Generate and route partner operations checklists without adding unnecessary human-review gates.
- Keep checklist items specific to the supplied partner type and requested setup requirements.
- Treat compensation model and commission checklist items as descriptive records only. Do not approve compensation, calculate payouts, decide legal eligibility, or create binding terms.
- Do not activate a partner or claim that onboarding is complete unless the supplied completed items support that status.
- Do not request, expose, or forward private borrower records. Forward only allowlisted partner request fields; exclude `shared_secret`.
- Keep funding language educational. Never promise approval, rates, terms, lender acceptance, or funding.
