# Partner Outreach Email Templates

These are reusable drafts, not proof of prior contact, program terms, or results. Fill placeholders from supplied non-sensitive context. The production handler uses controlled variants of these patterns; its full contract is in `actions/no-auth/partner-outreach-campaign-actions.md`.

## Professional-services introduction

**Subject:** A referral conversation for {{company_name}}

Hello {{contact_name}},

Would funding-readiness education be useful to the {{target_client_type}} you serve in {{niche}}? A possible starting point is a client-controlled introduction that preserves your advisory role and follows agreed communication preferences.

Would a short process overview be useful?

## Merchant, payroll, or equipment channel

**Subject:** An optional referral step for your service journey

Hi {{contact_name}},

Could an optional funding conversation help address questions that arise during your service or purchase process? We could compare where an introduction would fit and how to keep it useful and respectful.

Would a short conversation about the handoff process be useful?

## Warm relationship

**Subject:** Exploring a referral process together

Hi {{contact_name}},

Could we build on our connection by comparing the client situations where an introduction might be useful? We can discuss responsibilities, confidentiality expectations, and follow-up preferences.

Would a brief call next week work?

## Inactive or former partner

**Subject:** Revisiting our referral conversation

Hi {{contact_name}},

I wanted to reconnect and learn what would be useful now. Would it make sense to revisit whether a referral conversation fits your current priorities?

## Follow-up sequence

- Day 3 from first touch: Would a short overview of a client-controlled introduction be useful?
- Day 7 from first touch: Should I close the loop for now, or is there a better time to reconnect?

Stop on reply or opt-out. Respect existing authorization, channel permissions, and contact preferences. For multi-channel drafts, start with email, then suggest LinkedIn and email only where permitted. Do not infer SMS consent from a phone number or from campaign generation.

Event outreach may check interest in a future educational session. Add actual event details only after they are known. Never invent approved program terms, lender outcomes, testimonials, or past interactions. Internal notes and personalization context do not belong in the sent message.
