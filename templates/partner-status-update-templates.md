# Partner Status Update Templates

The status Action uses these bounded message forms. Keep partner updates concise and do not add financial or underwriting detail.

| Stage | Safe message |
| --- | --- |
| Referral received | We received the referral. |
| Borrower contacted | We have connected with the business owner. |
| Intake in progress | Intake is in progress. |
| Documents pending | We are waiting on requested information. |
| Lender-fit review | The scenario is being reviewed for potential routing options. |
| Options under review | Potential options are being reviewed. |
| Additional information needed | Additional information is needed before the process can continue. |
| Closed or withdrawn | The process is complete. |

Example wrapper:

> Hi Alex, we are waiting on requested information. We will share another update when there is a partner-safe status change.

This is a draft, not a promise of lender acceptance, approval, or funding. Confirm recipient and consent basis before sending.
