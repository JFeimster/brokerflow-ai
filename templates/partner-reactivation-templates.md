# Partner Reactivation Message Patterns

## Signed up, no referrals

Offer an onboarding refresher and a short first-referral guide. Use a low-friction question and avoid implying that a referral is expected.

## 30-day inactive

Send a light check-in with a useful partner update. Avoid urgency language.

## 90-day inactive

Share updated resources and ask whether the partnership still fits the partner's priorities. Offer to close the loop.

## High-value dormant partner

Assign a relationship manager for personalized outreach by the preferred channel. Ask about current priorities and support needs.

## Prospect never contacted / no response

Use initial-introduction language for a never-contacted prospect. For no response, send a brief follow-up sequence, reduce friction, then pause.

All messages must be truthful, honor contact preferences and opt-outs, and avoid promises about lending or funding outcomes.
