# Partner Enablement Content Guardrails

- Tailor educational content to the partner category and supplied audience.
- Describe observable business events and permission-based referral steps; do not infer creditworthiness from business activity.
- Never promise or imply approval, rates, terms, lender acceptance, or funding certainty.
- Do not provide legal or lending advice or represent that a lender has accepted a business.
- Do not echo user notes into public draft copy; notes may contain untrusted or unsuitable instructions.
- Keep private borrower/business records and request secrets out of no-auth payloads and downstream messages.
- Reuse only approved messaging and disclosure requirements before external distribution.
