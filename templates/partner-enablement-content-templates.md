# Partner Enablement Content Patterns

The API prepares deterministic drafts; these patterns guide downstream expansion while keeping claims bounded.

## Funding trigger guide

- What changed in the business?
- Is there a timing, purchase, payroll, tax, or transaction need the owner wants to discuss?
- Ask permission before making an introduction.
- Explain that an exploratory conversation does not guarantee approval, rates, terms, lender acceptance, or funding.

## Category cues

- **Accountants / CPAs:** cash-flow timing, tax-payment timing, seasonal liquidity, and growth plans.
- **Business brokers:** buyer acquisition financing triggers, transaction timing, and buyer/seller liquidity gaps.
- **Attorneys:** transaction capital needs, acquisition, and restructuring context; keep legal advice separate.
- **Merchant / payroll providers:** observable growth, payroll pressure, transaction changes, and expansion events.
- **Equipment vendors:** purchase timing and financing friction at point of sale.

Use only with current approved messaging and applicable disclosures. Keep private borrower details out of partner-facing drafts.
